Attic insulation grants in Ireland

For a detached house, the SEAI attic insulation grant is €2,000 at the standard rate or €2,500 at the enhanced rate for eligible applicants.

Who can apply

The home needs an MPRN and must have been built and occupied before 2011. The enhanced rate has specific conditions for eligible first-time buyers, recipients of qualifying welfare payments, and properties in active Housing Assistance Payment or Rental Accommodation Scheme arrangements.

Before work starts

  1. Choose an SEAI-registered contractor for attic insulation.
  2. Apply to SEAI online or by post.
  3. Get grant approval before starting the work. Accept your offer within 30 days.

Allow for the work, post-works Building Energy Rating assessment and paperwork within eight months of the offer.
