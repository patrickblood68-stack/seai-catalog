- Type: AMOLED

- Refresh rate max: 144 Hz
- Peak brightness: 2600 nits

- Rear telephoto resolution: 50 MP

## Front Camera

- Front resolution: 20 MP

## Battery & Charging

- Typical capacity: 5000 mAh
- Wired charging max: 120 W

- Wifi: Wi-Fi 7 / Wi-Fi 6E / Wi-Fi 6
- Bluetooth version: 5.4
- Cellular: 5G NR, 4G LTE, 3G and 2G
