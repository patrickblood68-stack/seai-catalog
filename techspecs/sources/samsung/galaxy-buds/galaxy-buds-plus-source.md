## Battery

- Earbud capacity: 85 mAh
- Case capacity: 270 mAh
- Listening per charge: Up to 11 hours
- Listening with case: Up to 22 hours
- Talk time per charge: Up to 7.5 hours
- Talk time with case: Up to 15 hours

## Charging

- Wireless charging: Yes
- Quick charge: 3 minutes for 1 hour of playback

## Dimensions

- Earbud dimensions: 17.5 × 19.2 × 22.5 mm
- Earbud weight: 6.3 g
- Case dimensions: 38.8 × 70.0 × 26.5 mm
- Case weight: 39.6 g

## Connectivity

- Bluetooth Version: 5.0

## Audio

- Drivers: Two-way dynamic, woofer and tweeter
- Microphones: Two outer and one inner per earbud
- Codecs: SBC, AAC and Samsung Scalable Codec

## Durability

- IP Rating: IPX2
